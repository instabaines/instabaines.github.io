Subgraph mining often sounds academic, but it plays a critical role in how we uncover
repeating structure, behavior, and intent in complex systems such as social networks,
transaction graphs, biological networks, and knowledge graphs.

In this article, I explain **what subgraph mining is**, **why it matters**, and **how it
translates into real-world business and research impact**, using intuitive examples rather
than heavy theory.
