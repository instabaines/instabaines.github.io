Research profile

I am an AI researcher and engineer working at the intersection of Computational Social Systems, Network Science, and Temporal Modeling. My work focuses on understanding how information and narratives propagate across complex systems using graph-based learning, scalable machine learning, and production-oriented AI systems.

My research places particular emphasis on collective structures, motifs, communities, and interaction patterns rather than isolated nodes. I study how these structures emerge, evolve, and shape the dynamics of information spread, influence, ranking, and coordination in networked environments.
